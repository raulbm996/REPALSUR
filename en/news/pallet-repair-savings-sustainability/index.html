The importance of pallet repair in the logistics chain

Repairing pallets not only saves costs but also reduces environmental impact. Discover how our repair service extends the useful life of your pallets and optimizes your company's resources.

The pallet repair service is one of the smartest solutions for companies looking to optimize their logistics resources. Instead of discarding damaged pallets, repairing them allows their lifespan to be significantly extended.

What kind of damage can be repaired?

The most common damages to pallets include broken boards, detached blocks, protruding nails, and deformations due to overloading. At Repalsur, we have a specialized team that evaluates each pallet and determines the most appropriate repair.

Repair process

Our process includes the visual and functional inspection of the pallet, the replacement of damaged parts with ones in perfect condition, the renailing of loose elements, and a final resistance check. All this is done with professional machinery and quality materials.

Cost savings

Repairing a pallet can cost up to 60% less than purchasing a new one. For companies handling large volumes, this saving translates into thousands of euros a year.

Positive environmental impact

Every repaired pallet is a pallet that does not end up in the landfill. Furthermore, it avoids the consumption of new wood and the energy associated with manufacturing. It is a clear commitment to sustainability and environmental responsibility.
