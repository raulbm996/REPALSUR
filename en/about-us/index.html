Our history

A lifetime with wood

With over 30 years of experience, at Repalsur we have dedicated a lifetime to the recycling of wooden pallets, as well as their manufacturing, treatment, and distribution for company logistics.

Our beginnings were forged in an environment marked by the need for efficient and sustainable solutions in the world of packaging and transport, and since then we have not stopped growing or adapting our services to market demands.

Over these three decades, we have established ourselves as professionals in constant evolution. We understand that each client requires personalized attention and custom-designed solutions.

Our commitment

Logistics that cares for the environment

We know how important it is for companies to have a service that not only meets their logistics needs but also contributes to environmental protection.

We take responsibility for applying sustainable practices, promoting the circular economy, and reducing ecological impact. We work with a customer-oriented approach: fast, personalized solutions, durable products, and a reliable service.

A pallet, many lives

Circular economy

The pallet you throw away, we recover it

With over 30 years in wooden pallet recycling, at Repalsur we close the loop: less new wood, less waste, and lower costs for your logistics.

  1. 1We collect

    We come to your company to collect used pallets, quickly and on schedule.

  2. 2We sort

    Each pallet is inspected: direct reuse, repair, or recycling.

  3. 3We repair

    We replace damaged boards and blocks to extend their lifespan.

  4. 4Back on the road

    They return to your warehouse or to another company's, with our own fleet.
