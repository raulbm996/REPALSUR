01 · Service

Recovery

Collection and recovery of used pallets to give them a second life.

We collect used pallets at our customers' facilities. Through a rigorous selection process, we classify them according to their condition and allocate them for repair, recycling, or direct reuse.

  • We actively contribute to the circular economy and waste reduction
  • Fast and scheduled collection according to your needs
  • Cost-effective service that optimizes your logistics costs
Request a quote

02 · Service

Buying and selling

Purchase and sale of high-quality, inspected used pallets.

We buy and sell used pallets in large quantities. Each pallet is inspected and sorted to ensure it meets the quality standards necessary for reuse in demanding logistics processes.

  • Rigorous quality inspection on every unit
  • Large stock available in various sizes and types
  • Competitive prices and flexible conditions
Request a quote

03 · Service

Repair

We repair and recondition damaged pallets to extend their useful life.

Repairing pallets is the best solution to save costs and protect the environment. In our workshop, we replace damaged boards and blocks and recondition each pallet so it can work safely again.

  • Savings compared to buying new pallets
  • We extend the lifespan of your pallets
  • Less waste and less new wood
Request a quote

04 · Service

Distribution

Pallet distribution and logistics adapted to each client.

We have our own fleet that allows us to make fast and efficient deliveries throughout the region. Our service adapts to the timeframes and volumes you need, guaranteeing punctuality and reliability.

  • Fast deliveries with our own fleet
  • Coverage throughout Andalusia and surrounding areas
  • Scheduled and on-demand deliveries
